Do I need any experience?+

No experience is needed for Barista Essentials. We start with the fundamentals and build your confidence through practice. Contact us if you’re unsure which course fits.

When do classes take place?+

Classes run Monday to Saturday. Morning sessions are 8:00am–1:30pm and evening sessions are 2:00pm–5:30pm. Contact the academy to confirm the next intake.

Where is the academy?+

You’ll find us on Bukoto – Kisaasi Road in Kampala, Uganda. Call +256 702 942 503 for directions before visiting.

How do I join?+

Choose your course and complete the enquiry form. You can then send your details to the academy by WhatsApp or email to confirm availability and registration. The registration fee is UGX 50,000.
